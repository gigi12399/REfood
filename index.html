Why REfood

Transparent, affordable, and built for Bangkok

Exact item listings

"No surprise. You choose what you save." See precisely what's in every listing — never a blind surprise bag.

Dynamic discounts

Prices scale from 20–80% off depending on how close an item is to its expiry time.

Dietary & allergen tags

Vegetarian, halal, and allergen flags on every listing, so you can filter for what matters to you.

Rescue Score & rewards

Earn points on every rescue, climb Bronze → Silver → Gold tiers, and build a 7-day rescue streak.

Follow your favorite shops

Get notified the moment a shop you follow posts new surplus — no more checking back and forth.

Pickup or delivery

Grab it yourself for free, or get it delivered by REfood's own riders — not a 3rd-party service.
